KOREAN GRAMMAR

🔤 Korean Particles

Learn how Korean words connect to make sentences.

은 / 는 — Topic Particle

은 and 는 show the topic of a sentence.

저는 학생이에요.

Jeoneun haksaeng-ieyo.

I am a student.

Mimi ni mwanafunzi.

이 / 가 — Subject Particle

이 and 가 identify the subject of a sentence.

친구가 와요.

Chinguga wayo.

A friend is coming.

Rafiki anakuja.

을 / 를 — Object Particle

을 and 를 are used after the object of an action.

밥을 먹어요.

Babeul meogeoyo.

I eat rice / food.

Ninakula chakula.

에 — Time / Place / Direction

에 can indicate a destination or a specific time.

학교에 가요.

Hakgyoe gayo.

I go to school.

Ninaenda shuleni.

에서 — Place of Action

에서 shows where an action takes place.

학교에서 공부해요.

Hakgyoeseo gongbuhaeyo.

I study at school.

Ninasoma shuleni.

의 — Possession

의 can show possession or relationship.

친구의 책이에요.

Chingue chaegieyo.

It is my friend's book.

Ni kitabu cha rafiki yangu.

도 — Also / Too

도 means also or too.

저도 학생이에요.

Jeodo haksaeng-ieyo.

I am also a student.

Mimi pia ni mwanafunzi.

와 / 과 — And / With

와 and 과 can connect nouns or show "with".

친구와 공부해요.

Chinguwa gongbuhaeyo.

I study with a friend.

Ninasoma na rafiki.
