GRAMMAR

Build Korean Sentences

Korean sentence structure becomes easier when you understand the particles and patterns.

은 / 는

Topic Particle

Used to introduce or emphasize the topic of a sentence.

저는 학생이에요.
I am a student.
이 / 가

Subject Particle

Marks the subject of a Korean sentence.

친구가 와요.
A friend is coming.
을 / 를

Object Particle

Marks the object receiving the action.

밥을 먹어요.
I eat rice.
